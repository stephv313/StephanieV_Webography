Modern Advancements in Video Games



URL The Strong National Museum of Play
Site Author The Strong National Museum of Play
Abstract The Strong National Museum of Play's Video Game History Timeline explains how video games have changed over the years, beginning with early developments in the 1940s and continuing into modern gaming. The timeline goes over important inventions, gaming systems, and popular games that helped shape the video game industry. It discusses the growing popularity of arcade games during the 1970s and 1980s, along with the development of home gaming consoles that allowed people to enjoy video games outside of arcades. The timeline also highlights the release of popular games such as Pac-Man, Donkey Kong, and Tetris, as well as Nintendo's introduction of the Nintendo Entertainment System and Game Boy. As new gaming systems were introduced, video games became more accessible and offered different ways for people to play. This source provides a chronological look at the major developments in video game history and how gaming continued to grow as technology improved.




URL Museum of Computer Games (Saint Petersburg, Russia)
Site Author Museum of Computer Games, Saint Petersburg
Abstract The Museum of Computer Games' Consoles page explains how home gaming consoles developed over time and how they changed the way people played video games. The page begins with the release of the Magnavox Odyssey in 1972, the first home gaming console, and continues with the development of systems such as the Atari 2600 and Nintendo Entertainment System. It discusses how interchangeable game cartridges allowed players to enjoy a larger variety of games without needing to purchase an entirely new console. As home gaming became more popular, games also began to change, offering longer gameplay, larger environments, and more advanced features. The page also goes over improvements in gaming technology throughout the 1980s and 1990s, including the shift from 8-bit to 16-bit graphics and eventually the introduction of 3D graphics with Sony's PlayStation. These developments show how home gaming consoles continued to improve and offered players new ways to experience video games.
